## Status key

- **M** measured: real run on real hardware, artifact kept
- **D** modelled: fitted model, simulator or analysis; validation stated
- **E** estimated: reasoned guess; the text says so every time
- **U** unsupported: no evidence yet; must become M, D or E, or be cut
- **C** cited: stated by prior work; the citation is the evidence
